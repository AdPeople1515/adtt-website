Common Questions

Interior film Singapore — FAQ

How much does interior film cost in Singapore?

Interior film is priced per square metre, depending on total wall/surface area, the finish chosen (wood grain, marble, metallic) and surface preparation needed. ADTT Projects provides an exact written quotation after a free site survey.

Is interior film durable? How long does it last?

Premium interior film from our TGA-certified range is rated for 5–10 years under normal indoor conditions. It is scratch-resistant, moisture-resistant and suitable for high-traffic areas including kitchens and bathrooms.

Can interior film be applied over existing walls and furniture without demolition?

Yes. Interior film is applied directly onto existing walls, cabinets and furniture surfaces — no hacking or demolition required, and business or home life continues with minimal disruption.

How long does interior film installation take?

A single room or wall section is typically completed in a few hours to a day. Larger projects such as full lift interior transformations are usually completed overnight with zero disruption to building operations.
