The Mechanism

How PDLC Smart Film Works

A thin layer of liquid crystal droplets, laminated onto your existing glass. What happens next depends on whether power is flowing through it.

Frosted

Power Off

Liquid crystals scatter in random directions. Light scatters with them — the glass turns opaque. Instant privacy, zero electricity.

Clear

Power On

A low-voltage current aligns every crystal the same way. Light passes straight through — clear in about one second.

  • Wiring must be connected to the transformer/switcher before the film can switch — it won't work unpowered until installed
  • Works on existing glass — no window, door, or partition replacement
  • Frosted mode draws no power — safe to leave off overnight
  • Doubles as a projector screen when frosted, since light diffuses evenly

Behind The Job

How Installation Actually Works

A real ADTT Projects installation, start to finish — no demolition, no mess, done on-site.

Step 1

Film arrives as a roll and is measured against the exact glass panel dimensions from your site survey.

Step 2

Applied directly onto your existing glass, panel by panel — no need to remove or replace the glass itself.

Step 3

Film is smoothed and sealed edge to edge for a bubble-free, seamless finish.

Step 4

Wired to the transformer and tested with the remote — ready to switch on the spot.

More Applications

Does It Work On My Glass? Two more shapes, same film.

Curved & Shaped Windows

Yes — smart film applies to curved and irregularly shaped glass, not just flat panels. Tightly curved sections may need extra measurement and seam planning, confirmed during your site survey.

Sliding Doors

Smart film applies to sliding glass doors too — installed panel by panel, so each pane switches independently and the sliding mechanism and handles are completely unaffected. Examples below of the black-framed sliding door glass this works on.

Folding Doors

Smart film also works on folding/bi-fold door glass — each panel switches the same way as a fixed window, without affecting how the door folds or operates.
